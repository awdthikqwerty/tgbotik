# Story Engine

**Purpose:** turn the premise into an original script and timed storyboard for Mini Shorts or Full Stories.

- **Reads:** `production/creative-direction.md`, brief, research findings, cast constraints.
- **Owns:** `production/SCRIPT.md` and `production/storyboard.json` (start from `templates/SCRIPT_example.md` and `templates/storyboard.json`).
- **Handoff:** beat purpose, frame bounds, narration/dialogue text and language, shot IDs, continuity and transition intent → Research Engine, Character Director, Animation Engine, Camera Director, Audio Studio.
- **Contract:** `creative/contracts.ts` (`Beat`, `ProductionBrief`). Timing becomes locked before final audio cues.
