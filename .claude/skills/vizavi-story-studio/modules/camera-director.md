# Camera Director

**Purpose:** create a varied, motivated shot grammar and transitions that clarify the story.

- **Reads:** selected visual treatments, storyboard timing, scene layout and camera-safe zones.
- **Owns:** camera curves, composition, focus/DOF, cuts/transitions, and shot notes in `production/storyboard.json` and project-local `story.ts`.
- **Handoff:** frame-accurate shot/camera table and transition cues → Animation Engine and Audio Studio.
- **Rule:** avoid repeated shot sizes/moves/transitions unless a deliberate callback needs them; use intentional holds when they serve the beat.
- **Contract:** `creative/contracts.ts` (`ShotPlan`).
