# Research Engine

**Purpose:** verify claims in historical, scientific, or otherwise factual stories.

- **Reads:** script claims and story context.
- **Owns:** `production/research-notes.md` with claim, source URL, source type, date/access date, confidence, and dramatization boundary.
- **Handoff:** sourced/corrected claims and pronunciation notes → Story Engine and Audio Studio.
- **Rule:** use primary/authoritative sources where possible; distinguish source fact from inferred or fictional staging. Skip for fully fictional stories.
- **Contract:** `creative/contracts.ts` (`ResearchFinding`).
